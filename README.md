# Career Compass AI

Build a premium AI-powered career platform called InternHub AI

Tagline

Know Yourself. Verify the Opportunity. Build Your Career.

Vision

This is NOT an internship marketplace.

Do NOT build another Internshala clone.

InternHub AI is an AI-powered Internship Intelligence Platform for Tier-2 and Tier-3 college students in India.

The platform helps students answer four questions:

 Am I ready for internships?

 What exactly should I improve?

 Is this internship worth my time?

 How can I prove to my college that I'm doing a genuine internship?

The design should feel premium, minimal, modern, and startup-ready.

Use dark mode with blue/purple gradients, glassmorphism, clean spacing, rounded cards, smooth animations, and dashboard-style UI inspired by Linear, Notion, Stripe, and Perplexity.

Do not clutter the interface.

Only four major features should exist.

Important

This platform should not look like an AI wrapper around ChatGPT.

Every report should feel structured, professional, actionable, and personalized.

The user should feel like they received a report from an experienced career coach, recruiter, placement officer, and hiring manager combined—not just generic AI advice.

One last recommendation

I'd rename Resume Analyzer to Resume Intelligence and Career Assessment to AI Career Audit.

Those names sound more premium and signal that the output goes beyond a simple score. They also reinforce your positioning as an internship intelligence platform, not just another collection of AI tools.
